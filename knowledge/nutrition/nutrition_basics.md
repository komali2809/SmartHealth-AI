# Nutrition Basics

## Purpose

This document provides general nutrition information for SmartHealth AI.
It is intended for educational and wellness purposes.

## Macronutrients

### Protein

Protein contributes to normal growth and maintenance of body tissues.
Common food sources include lentils, beans, dairy products, eggs,
soy foods, nuts, seeds, fish, and meat.

### Carbohydrates

Carbohydrates are an important source of energy.
Sources include rice, oats, wheat, millet, fruits, vegetables,
and legumes.

### Dietary Fat

Dietary fat provides energy and supports normal physiological functions.
Sources include nuts, seeds, vegetable oils, dairy products,
and some fish.

## Fiber

Dietary fiber is found in foods such as vegetables, fruits,
whole grains, legumes, nuts, and seeds.

A varied diet containing fiber-rich foods can support normal
digestive health.

## Hydration

Water is essential for normal body functions.
Individual fluid requirements vary depending on factors such as
activity, climate, food intake, and individual circumstances.

## Balanced Eating

A balanced eating pattern can include:

- Vegetables
- Fruits
- Whole grains
- Legumes
- Protein-rich foods
- Appropriate sources of dietary fat
- Adequate fluids

Food requirements differ between individuals.

## SmartHealth AI Safety

Nutrition values displayed by SmartHealth AI are estimates based
on the application's datasets and calculations.

They should not be treated as personalized medical prescriptions.
