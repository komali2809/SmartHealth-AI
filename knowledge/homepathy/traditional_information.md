# Homeopathy Traditional Information

## Purpose

This document provides general educational information about
homeopathy as a traditional health practice.

It is not a medical treatment recommendation.

## Traditional Context

Homeopathy is a system of traditional medicine that uses
highly diluted preparations.

Different practitioners may follow different approaches.

## Evidence and Safety

Claims about the effectiveness of homeopathic products for
treating diseases should not be assumed to be established
medical facts.

Users should not replace prescribed medical treatment with
homeopathic products.

Some products marketed as homeopathic may contain ingredients
that can cause adverse effects or interact with other products.

## Professional Care

People experiencing persistent, severe, or rapidly worsening
symptoms should seek appropriate medical evaluation.

Emergency symptoms require appropriate emergency medical care.

## SmartHealth AI Safety

SmartHealth AI presents homeopathy information only as
traditional and educational reference material.

The application does not diagnose disease or recommend
homeopathic treatment as a replacement for professional care.
