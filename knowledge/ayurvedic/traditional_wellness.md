# Ayurveda Traditional Wellness Information

## Purpose

This document contains general informational material about
Ayurveda as a traditional system of health and wellness.

The information is provided for educational purposes.

## Traditional Perspective

Ayurvedic traditions may discuss concepts related to:

- Diet
- Daily routines
- Sleep
- Physical activity
- Herbal preparations
- Traditional wellness practices

Different Ayurvedic traditions and practitioners may interpret
these concepts differently.

## Food and Lifestyle

Traditional Ayurvedic approaches may emphasize individualized
food choices, daily routines, moderation, and attention to
lifestyle habits.

These concepts should be understood as traditional wellness
information rather than established medical treatment.

## Herbal Products

Herbal products can have biological effects and may interact
with medicines or medical conditions.

Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional before
using herbs or supplements for a health condition.

## SmartHealth AI Safety

SmartHealth AI does not claim that Ayurvedic practices or products
cure or treat diseases.

Ayurvedic information displayed by the application is intended
for traditional and educational reference only.

For symptoms that are severe, persistent, or worsening, users
should seek appropriate professional medical care.
